FIBSF 2023 — Toulouse

March 30–31, 2023 · TBS Education

The 2023 French Inter-Business School Finance Workshop was organized by Pierre Mella-Barral and the Finance, Economics and Econometrics Lab at TBS and hosted by TBS Education, 20 boulevard Lascrosses, Toulouse (Room A327). The workshop opened with a welcome address by Stéphanie Lavigne, Dean of TBS Education. Presentation slots were 20 minutes, discussions 5 minutes. Registration, attendance and meals were free of charge.
