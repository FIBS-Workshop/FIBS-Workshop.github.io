IBSFS 2022 — Sophia Antipolis

March 31 – April 1, 2022 · SKEMA Business School · 7th Inter Business-School Finance Seminar

After a year's interruption due to Covid-19, the 7th edition of the seminar was organized by Armin Schwienbacher and Helen Bollaert and hosted by SKEMA Business School on its Sophia-Antipolis campus (60 rue Fedor Dostoïevski, Valbonne). Twelve papers were presented over two half-days — 20-minute presentations followed by a 7-minute discussion and questions from the audience — with participants from EDHEC, emlyon, ESCP, ESSEC, Grenoble EM, HEC Paris, Montpellier BS, NEOMA, Paris-Dauphine, SKEMA, and TBS. The workshop dinner was held at L'Amandier in Mougins.
