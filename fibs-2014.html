IBSFC 2014 — Grenoble

February 6–7, 2014 · Grenoble École de Management · 4th Inter-Business School Finance Conference

The 4th Inter-Business School Finance Conference — an early edition of what would become the FIBS workshop — was organized by François Desmoulins-Lebeault and hosted by Grenoble École de Management. Each paper received a 30-minute presentation, a 10-minute review, and 5 minutes of collective discussion. Paper titles for this edition are not archived; the program below lists presenters and reviewers.
