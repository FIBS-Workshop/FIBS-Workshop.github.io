Past Editions

Fifteen years of inter-business school finance meetings

The workshop began in Lyon in 2011 as the Inter-School Conference in Finance, organized in turn by the participating schools. After a pause between 2015 and 2018, it was revived in Reims in 2019; the 2021 edition could not take place because of the Covid-19 pandemic. Archived programs are linked below where available.
