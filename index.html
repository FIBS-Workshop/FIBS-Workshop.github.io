About the Workshop

The French Inter-Business School Workshop in Finance (FIBS) brings together, once a year, faculty members from the finance departments of French business schools and partner universities. Over two half-days, participants present and discuss work in progress in all areas of finance — asset pricing, corporate finance, sustainable finance, market microstructure, financial econometrics, and beyond.

The format is deliberately simple and collegial: each paper receives a dedicated presentation slot followed by a prepared discussion by a colleague from another institution. The workshop is hosted by a different school each year, and attendance is open to faculty members of the participating institutions beyond the presenters and discussants themselves.

The workshop carries on a tradition that began in Lyon in 2011 as the Inter-School Conference in Finance, with early editions hosted in Rouen (2012), Paris – La Défense (ESSEC, 2013) and Grenoble (GEM, 2014). Revived in Reims in 2019 (NEOMA), it has since been held in Lyon (emlyon, 2020; then no conference in 2021 because of Covid), Sophia Antipolis (SKEMA, 2022), Toulouse (TBS, 2023), Montpellier (MBS, 2024), Lyon (emlyon, 2025) and Nantes (Audencia, 2026) — see the full list of past editions. The 2027 edition will be hosted by Université Paris Dauphine – PSL on its Tunis campus.
