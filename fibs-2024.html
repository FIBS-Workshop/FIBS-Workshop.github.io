FIBS 2024 — Montpellier

April 25–26, 2024 · Montpellier Business School

The 2024 French Inter-Business School Workshop in Finance was organized and hosted by Montpellier Business School, from Thursday April 25th after lunch until Friday April 26th before lunch, in Room C2, Building A, 2300 avenue des Moulins, Montpellier.
